# MobileIPTV privacy policy

Effective 30 September 2026. Applies to MobileIPTV for Android, from Google Play or from the MobileIPTV-releases page, and to the PerchTV receiver it pairs with.

MobileIPTV is a player. It includes no channels, playlists or other content, and it has no accounts, advertising or analytics. Most of what it knows stays on your phone.

## What stays on your phone

- The sources you add: addresses, usernames and passwords, and the catalog and guide imported from them.
- Your settings, favourites, watch history and positions.
- A diagnostic log of what the app did, with passwords and tokens removed. It is kept on the phone and deleted as it rotates.

We never receive any of this unless you choose to send it, as described below.

## What leaves your phone, and where it goes

| What | Where it goes | When |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Source address, username and password | The server of the provider you added | Every time the app loads that source's catalog, guide or a stream |
| Film and series titles | The Movie Database (TMDB), to fetch artwork and descriptions | When you open a title's details |
| Plex sign-in | plex.tv and your own Plex server | When you add a Plex server |
| The stream you cast, and its request headers | Your paired television, through our pairing service | When you cast. It is encrypted on your phone and can only be read by the television; our service cannot read it |
| Your device's model name and a random pairing id | Our pairing service | When you pair a television, so it can show which phone is connected |
| A diagnostic log and your settings, passwords removed, with the subject and description you type | Our reporting service, which stores it in a private repository only the developer can read | Only when you tap Report an issue |

"Send log and backup" does not send anything to us. It opens Android's share sheet, and the file goes to whichever app and person you choose. A backup includes passwords only if you choose that when exporting.

Our reporting service also keeps a random id for your installation for ten minutes, to limit how many reports one phone can send.

## What we do not do

- We do not sell or share your data, or use it for advertising.
- We do not collect your location, contacts, photos or files.
- The camera is used only to scan a television's pairing code, when you tap Scan the code. No image leaves your phone.

## Keeping and deleting your data

Data on your phone is removed when you delete a source, clear the log in Settings, or uninstall the app. To have a problem report you sent deleted, open an issue at [github.com/rkinnc75/MobileIPTV-releases/issues](https://github.com/rkinnc75/MobileIPTV-releases/issues) giving the date you sent it; we will delete it.

## Your content

You are responsible for the sources you add and for having the right to watch them.

## Children

MobileIPTV is not directed at children.
